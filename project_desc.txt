This combat sports hub solves the problem of dispersed information by providing a one-stop destination for fight fans to interact with live schedules, results, and analysis of UFC, boxing, and WWE. Targeted at combat sports enthusiasts aged 18-45 years, it combines an editorial blog with an interactive dashboard that features:

Future/past fight cards

Embedded media (PPV highlights, podcasts)

Fighter statistics

Cross-promotion event calendars
The product merges unique commentary (blog posts, fight breakdowns) with data-driven analysis (trend lines, prediction models) in a manner that differs from standard news aggregators by adding personalized content recommendations and community voting features.
